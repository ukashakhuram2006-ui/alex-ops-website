About Alex Ops

Built around HVAC operations, not AI novelty.

Alex Ops is an HVAC revenue operations platform for independent contractors. It is designed to keep revenue-critical work visible across the lead-to-cash lifecycle while working around the software a contractor already uses.

What Alex Ops covers

Alex Ops treats call intake as one part of a broader operating system. Its product direction spans lead capture, appointment management, missed-call recovery, dispatch, open work, estimate recovery, job closeout, parts and warranty work, accounts receivable, maintenance memberships, reviews, capacity and related operating handoffs.

Operating principle:

Detect what is stuck, decide the next action using the contractor's rules, then recommend, request approval for, or automate the permitted action. The objective is simple: nothing falls through the cracks.

Founder

Ukasha Khuram

Ukasha is the founder and builder of Alex Ops. His work focuses on AI automation and operational systems that reduce manual follow-up and keep important HVAC work visible from first inquiry through closeout and collection.

Alex Ops is being developed with an overlay-first approach: identify one meaningful operating gap, keep people in control of sensitive actions, prove the workflow, and expand only where it makes operational sense.

Our category

We use the term HVAC Revenue Operations to describe the operating layer between acquiring a lead and actually collecting the revenue. The category includes the handoffs where calls, appointments, jobs, estimates, invoices and retention work can lose momentum.
