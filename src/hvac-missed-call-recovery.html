What Alex Ops is designed to do

Alex Ops treats missed-call recovery as part of the lead-to-job workflow. A missed inquiry can be surfaced as work that needs attention, routed according to the contractor's operating rules, and kept visible until the next permitted action is completed.

Why this matters for HVAC operations

HVAC demand is time-sensitive. A homeowner with a failed system may contact several contractors. A recovery process therefore needs more than a notification: the team needs context, ownership and a reliable follow-up state.

  • Surface missed inbound opportunities instead of leaving them buried in a phone log.
  • Keep the lead connected to customer, appointment and job context as it progresses.
  • Use recommend, approve or automatic modes depending on the action and business policy.
  • Preserve the contractor's existing software rather than forcing a rip-and-replace migration.

Start with the operating gap

Alex Ops is not positioned as a replacement phone system or a generic AI receptionist. Call intake and recovery are modules inside a broader HVAC Revenue Operations layer spanning leads, appointments, dispatch, jobs, estimates, collections and retention.
