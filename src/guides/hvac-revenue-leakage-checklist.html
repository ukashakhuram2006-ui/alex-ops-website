HVAC Revenue Leakage Checklist

Use this checklist to review whether every revenue-critical item has a visible state, an owner, a next action and an exception trigger when the next action does not happen.

Alex Ops Command Center. Real product interface shown with isolated fictional demo data.

1. Lead capture

  • Every inbound call, form or inquiry can become an identifiable lead.
  • Missed calls remain visible until a person or permitted workflow handles the next step.
  • Lead source and available customer context stay attached to the record.
  • Urgent inquiries can be distinguished from standard requests.
  • There is a clear owner for unresolved inbound opportunities.

2. Appointment and dispatch

  • Booked appointments have a visible status and responsible team member.
  • Unassigned or delayed work can be surfaced as an exception.
  • Dispatch decisions can use location, urgency, capacity and role constraints.
  • Changes do not silently remove the next required action.

3. Estimates and open opportunities

  • Open estimates have a defined follow-up state rather than relying on memory.
  • Unsold estimates can be surfaced when they exceed the business's follow-up threshold.
  • Ownership is preserved when an estimate moves between office and sales staff.
  • The team can distinguish waiting, declined, won and unresolved opportunities.

4. Job closeout

  • Completed field work has explicit closeout requirements.
  • Missing documentation, parts, warranty or invoice steps remain visible.
  • A job cannot disappear simply because the technician's field work is complete.
  • Exceptions have an owner and next permitted action.

5. Invoices and collections

  • Receivables that require attention can be identified by status or age.
  • Collection work has an owner and a recorded next action.
  • Customer communication follows the contractor's approved policy.
  • Paid, disputed and unresolved balances are distinguishable.

6. Retention

  • Review requests happen from an explicit completion state rather than staff memory.
  • Maintenance or membership opportunities can be surfaced at the appropriate point.
  • Customer history remains available when deciding the next action.

How to score the audit

For each item, mark clear, partially clear or unclear. Prioritize unclear handoffs that involve active customer demand, open estimates, incomplete work or money already owed. The objective is not to automate everything. First make ownership and the next action explicit; then decide whether that step should be recommended, approved by a person or automated.

Methodology and limitations

This checklist is the operating framework used in the design of Alex Ops. It is a process-audit tool, not an industry benchmark. It does not assign a universal dollar value to a missed call, estimate or invoice, and it does not claim that every contractor has the same workflow. Actual implementation depends on the contractor's software, data access, operating policies and permissions.
