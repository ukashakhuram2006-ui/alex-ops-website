HVAC estimate follow-up

Keep unsold HVAC estimates from going stale.

An estimate can be technically open while operationally forgotten. Alex Ops is designed to surface estimates that need attention and give the team a clear next action without replacing the field-service system that created them.

Turn an open estimate into owned work

A useful follow-up process needs state, timing, context and ownership. Alex Ops can organize those signals so a stale estimate becomes a visible exception rather than another row in a long list.

  • Identify estimates waiting beyond the business's chosen follow-up window.
  • Keep customer and job context attached to the opportunity.
  • Route the next action to the appropriate sales or office role.
  • Use human approval for actions that should not run automatically.
  • Keep follow-up status visible to owners and managers.

Automation should follow the sales process

Alex Ops uses recommend, approve and automatic operating modes. That makes it possible to begin conservatively: surface the right estimate, recommend the next step and let a person decide. More automation can be introduced only where the workflow is proven and permitted.

Part of HVAC Revenue Operations

Estimate recovery is one module in Alex Ops. The wider system is designed around the handoffs between lead capture, appointments, dispatch, jobs, estimates, closeout, collections and retention. The objective is simple: keep revenue-critical work from disappearing between systems and teams.
