FAQ

Questions HVAC owners ask first.

Is Alex Ops just an AI receptionist?

No. Call intake is one module. Alex Ops is an HVAC revenue-operations layer spanning lead capture, appointments, dispatch, jobs, estimates, collections and retention.

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. Alex Ops is overlay-first. The goal is to improve follow-through around your existing systems rather than force a rip-and-replace.

Does Alex Ops automatically take every action?

No. Workflows can run in recommend, approve or automatic modes so higher-risk actions stay under human control.

Can we start with one problem?

Yes. The preferred rollout is to identify one meaningful revenue leak, prove the workflow, then expand only where it makes operational sense.
