What the audit looks for

Lead capture gaps

Missed calls, inquiries or customer context that never reach a clear owner and next step.

Conversion gaps

Leads, appointments and open estimates that depend on memory or inconsistent manual follow-up.

Execution gaps

Open work, dispatch and closeout tasks that remain unresolved or lose ownership between roles.

Cash and retention gaps

Receivables, reviews and maintenance opportunities that are visible in a system but do not receive the next required action.

What you leave with

1. Leak map

A map of the lead-to-cash handoffs where opportunities can stall or disappear.

2. Priority list

The most important operational gaps ranked by urgency and practical impact.

3. First workflow

A recommended starting workflow based on the bottleneck uncovered rather than a generic feature bundle.

4. Implementation map

The systems, approvals, ownership and handoffs that would need to be confirmed before deployment.

Why Alex Ops starts with the handoff

HVAC businesses can already have scheduling, CRM, estimating and invoicing software while still losing visibility between those systems and the people using them. Alex Ops is designed around those operational exceptions. Its model is Detect → Decide → Act: identify work that is stuck or missing a next step, apply the contractor's rules, then recommend, request approval for, or automate the permitted action.

The audit does not promise a specific revenue lift and does not assume every workflow should be automated. Compatibility, data access and automation scope are confirmed during scoping.
