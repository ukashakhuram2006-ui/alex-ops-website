Collections is an operations workflow

The accounting system can record what is owed. The operational challenge is making sure the right person sees what needs attention, understands the context and knows the next step.

  • Surface overdue or attention-required receivables according to defined business rules.
  • Keep invoice follow-up connected to customer and job context.
  • Assign ownership instead of relying on memory or an unprioritized queue.
  • Use approval controls where customer communication requires human review.
  • Give owners and managers visibility into unresolved collection work.

Keep sensitive actions under control

Alex Ops supports recommend, approve and automatic modes. Collection workflows can therefore be designed around the contractor's policies rather than assuming every action should be automated. The appropriate mode and software connections are confirmed during implementation scoping.

From lead to cash

AR & Collections is one part of the broader Alex Ops model: Lead → Customer → Appointment → Job → Estimate/Invoice. The platform focuses on exceptions and handoffs across that lifecycle so work requiring attention does not quietly disappear.
