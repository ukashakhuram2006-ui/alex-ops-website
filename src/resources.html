How Alex Ops approaches the problem

Alex Ops uses a Detect → Decide → Act model. The objective is not to replace a contractor's existing field-service platform by default; it is to identify stuck or missing next steps, apply the contractor's operating rules and move permitted work forward with the appropriate level of human control.
